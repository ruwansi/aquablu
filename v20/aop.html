How AOP Technology Works

Understanding the science behind Advanced Oxidation Process

Hydroxyl Radical Generation

The system generates highly reactive hydroxyl radicals (•OH) through advanced oxidation processes, creating powerful oxidizing agents.

Contaminant Breakdown

Hydroxyl radicals attack and break down organic pollutants, bacteria, viruses, and other contaminants at the molecular level.

Pure Water Output

The process results in clean, safe water with eliminated contaminants and no harmful by-products or chemical residues.

AOP Process Flow

1
Water enters the AOP reactor system
2
Hydroxyl radicals are generated through oxidation
3
Contaminants are oxidized and neutralized
4
Clean, disinfected water is produced

The Science Behind AOP

Advanced Oxidation Processes generate hydroxyl radicals (•OH), which are among the most powerful oxidizing species available for water treatment. These radicals have an extremely high oxidation potential, making them capable of breaking down virtually any organic compound.

Unlike traditional chemical treatments, AOP doesn't introduce harmful substances into the water. Instead, it uses the power of oxidation to transform contaminants into harmless end products like water (H₂O) and carbon dioxide (CO₂).
